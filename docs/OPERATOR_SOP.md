# Operator SOP (pilot draft)

Who does what in Pedals Hub. Every step below leaves a timestamped audit-log entry. Policy values (7 days, 40%, ~20%) are team proposals until the institute approves them.

## Roles

| Role | In the app | Can | Cannot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hostel office / authorised officer** | Admin | Decide claims and disputes, issue clearance (approve / hold / deny), approve listings, record handovers and receipts, authorise dismantling, close salvage, process exit handovers, change policy, import data | — |
| **Pedals volunteers** | Auditor | Field audit, re-check, place notices, log reminders, grade, prepare draft listings, route to salvage review, log parts and residual | Clear ownership, decide claims, approve listings, record sales |
| **Partner mechanic** | Mechanic | Safety check, grading, itemised quotes, rate card | Any ownership or disposal decision |
| **Students** | Student app | Register, claim with evidence, semester check, reserve, sell / donate at exit | Anything in the admin area |

## Recover

1. **Audit (volunteers).** Tie the numbered tag, record the spot (bay, row, position, never a hostel name), take one timestamped photo, fill lock / rust / dust / blocked-in / sticker / main fault / grade. Never move, ride or unlock a cycle.
2. **Re-check (volunteers, 2+ days later).** Moved? → *In use*, stop. Tag missing → re-tag.
3. **Notice (volunteers).** Paper notice on the cycle and an approved hostel channel. Tick “physical notice placed”. The window opens.
4. **Reminder (volunteers).** Log the mid-way reminder by day 4.
5. **Claims (officer).** Compare the claimant’s description and proof with the audit record. Check a key or lock combination at the desk. Approve, ask for more, or mark disputed. Disputes go to the warden’s office.
6. **Clearance (officer).** Only after the window closes with no open claim. Write the reason. Expiry alone never clears a cycle.

## Reuse

7. **Grade and price (mechanic + volunteers).** A–D, faults, itemised regular quote (R), expected value after repair (V), as-is price (S), safety check. The R/V gate and the safety veto decide the route.
8. **List (volunteer drafts, officer approves).** Students see only approved listings. B/C carry “Requires repairs, not ride-ready”.
9. **Handover (desk).** Buyer pays the cycle price S only. Record the handover; the numbered receipt is generated. Repairs are paid by the buyer directly to any mechanic.
10. **Salvage (officer authorises, volunteers log).** Recover safe parts only: never brakes or frame/fork. Record the residual and wait for an authorised recycler; close with evidence.

## Prevent

11. **Semester check.** Open the window in Policy settings, put up the QR standee, log reminder rounds. Pending owners get a personal follow-up (desk or hostel rep), never removal.
12. **Graduation exit.** Verify the registered owner, schedule a desk handover, mark received. The custody receipt and clearance record are created; then grade before any listing.

## Handover between teams

* Records live in the app (in a pilot: on a hostel-office machine or server), not with a student group.
* Each semester: export the inventory + audit log CSV, brief the new volunteers on this SOP, and confirm who holds the admin role.
